What this afternoon is

Come as you are. Bring a friend.

An afternoon of country, folk and contemporary worship music, woven together with honest stories about where hope actually comes from. No ticket, no pressure, nothing to prepare. Just music worth hearing and a message worth carrying home.

The Music

Country, folk and contemporary worship — original songs alongside hymns you already know, with plenty you can sing along to.

The Stories

Honest, funny, real. The kind that put words to the hope behind the music instead of talking around it.

The Good News

A clear, unhurried word about Jesus — for anyone carrying something heavy, and anyone just curious.

“Be filled with the Spirit, singing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs from your heart to the Lord.”

Ephesians 5:18–20

Who you’ll hear

Three people, one afternoon

Becky Key
Singer & Songwriter

Becky’s music blends the warmth and storytelling of folk and country with the heart of contemporary worship. For more than 23 years she has shared music throughout California — in churches, community concerts and women’s conferences. She loves songs that bring people together, invite them to sing along, and leave them encouraged.

Joshua Vigran
Music Director, Zion Lutheran

Josh has been in music ministry since he was twelve, playing piano, bass, guitar, drums and organ in churches across California, New York, Texas and Alaska. He has been Zion’s Music Director since 2024, and was most recently collaborative pianist and instructor of piano at the University of Alaska Fairbanks. He is Becky’s brother, and writing and performing together is one of their greatest joys.

Pastor Bobby Key
Stories & Message

Bobby is a husband, dad of two girls, and a storyteller who believes hope is more than wishful thinking. For fifteen years he has helped people find the kind of hope that holds up in real life. His role here is simple: to put words to the hope behind the music, through honest stories and reflections that connect with everyday moments.
