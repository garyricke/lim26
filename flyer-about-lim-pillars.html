Lutheran Indian Ministries Native-Led · Christ-Centered · Since 1985

Three commitments.
One calling.

Lutheran Indian Ministries is a Native-led ministry sharing the Gospel with Native American and Alaska Native people. Our work rests on three pillars — each one supports the others.

Pillar 01

Proclaim

Sharing the Gospel in ways rooted in Native culture — honoring Native traditions as vessels of God’s grace. God speaks to Native hearts in Native ways.
In practiceWe travel by road, plane, boat, and snowmachine — going only where we are invited.
Pillar 02

Disciple

Developing Native leaders through Haskell LIGHT, our student center beside Haskell Indian Nations University — students from more than 100 tribes.
In practiceA home away from home: meals, Bible studies, mentorship, and the reminder that you don’t have to change to be loved by God.
Pillar 03

Heal

Scripture-based small groups for deep wounds — boarding schools, family separation, and generations of loss. Free to attend, always.
In practiceSessions at reservations, villages, cities, and online — including here in Alaska.

How we work

We don’t show up with answers. We arrive with open hearts. LIM is led by Native people who know the stories, the traditions, the culture, and the humor — and that changes everything.

For 500 years Native people were told to choose between being Native and being Christian. That choice was always a lie.

  • By invitation only — guided by tribal customs and deep respect.
  • Relationships, not programs — we build trust before anything else.
  • Native leaders sharing the Gospel in their own language and culture.
  • Full transparency — audited financials published every year.
