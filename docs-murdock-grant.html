Do we qualify?

  • 501(c)(3) public charity, at least three years oldLIM was founded in 1985. We need the IRS determination letter and the 509(a)/170(b) documentation.
  • Total project at least $200,000, request at least $100,000Any realistic three-year development-director budget clears both.
  • Faith-based organizations are welcomeThe Trust describes itself as “faith-informed” and funds ministries as well as secular organizations.
  • Based outside the NorthwestLIM is in Brookfield, Wisconsin. The Trust takes out-of-region organizations whose project brings at least 50% of its impact to Alaska, Washington, Oregon, Idaho or Montana. A Colorado group received $312,500 in 2026 for a development role serving Native youth in the Northwest, so it can be done. The role has to be clearly built around Northwest donors and the Alaska work.
  • LIM’s first fundraising position?The Trust funds only the first, and never a position that existed in the last five years. Has LIM ever had a paid development, fundraising or donor-relations role?
  • Not hired before the site visitAnyone hired before the Trust’s site visit (summer or fall 2027) isn’t eligible. So nobody gets hired for this role until a decision is made.

Sizing the ask: the math

Example only. Robert will supply the real salary. Say the role costs $110,000 a year fully loaded (salary, taxes, benefits, travel). The Trust pays 100% in year 1, 67% in year 2 and 33% in year 3, plus any one-time startup technology.

Joe’s figures, $500k from the Trust with $100k from LIM, would mean a role costing about $250k a year, and $500k a year in new gifts by year 4. That’s far above any development-staff grant in the Trust’s latest report.

Questions for Robert, needed before we write

  1. Fundraising history: has LIM ever had a paid development, fundraising or donor-relations role? When did it end?
  2. Numbers: annual operating budget, private gifts last year, and any government funding.
  3. The Northwest: roughly what share of LIM’s programs, participants and donors are in Alaska, Washington, Oregon, Idaho and Montana?
  4. The role: expected salary and benefits, and where the person would be based.
  5. Board: what share of the board gives, and what donor work is under way now? (Newsletter, events, major-donor visits.)
  6. Category: should we present LIM as Health (healing groups) or Faith Formation?
