What is a Healing Group?

Some wounds don’t heal on their own. Boarding schools. Family separation. Loss carried down through generations. A Healing Group is a small circle of people who sit with those wounds together, in the light of Scripture, and begin to set them down.

The curriculum comes from the American Bible Society’s Trauma Healing Institute. There is no pressure to share. You come as you are, and you take what is meaningful to you.

  • Always free — lunch included at in-person sessions
  • All are welcome, whatever your faith or background
  • Confidential — the group agrees to that together
  • Held in villages, on reservations, in cities, and online
