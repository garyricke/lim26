Who We Are

Where faith meets
culture and tradition.

A Native-led ministry sharing the Gospel with Native American and Alaska Native people — building relationships rooted in trust, healing, and hope.

We don’t show up with answers. We arrive with open hearts.

For 500 years Native people were told to choose between being Native and being Christian. We believe that choice was always a lie. God speaks to Native hearts in Native ways — and you don’t have to change to be loved by Him.

LIM is led by Native people who know the stories, the traditions, the culture, and the humor. We come only by invitation, following tribal customs and showing deep respect. We travel by road, plane, boat, and snowmachine to reach communities wherever they are.

Proclaim
Sharing the Gospel in ways rooted in Native culture — honoring Native traditions as vessels of God’s grace.
Disciple
Developing Native leaders through Haskell LIGHT, our student center beside Haskell Indian Nations University.
Heal
Scripture-based healing groups for the deep wounds of boarding schools, family separation, and generations of loss.
We serve Native and Alaska Native communities across the country — from city centers to villages off the road system.
