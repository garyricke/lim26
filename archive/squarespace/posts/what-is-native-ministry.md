# What is Native Ministry?

[News from Brookfield](/news-notes/category/News+from+Brookfield)

Jul 20

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/1665779778376-T5VXPU9LT7FZM66X03F3/image-asset.jpeg)

Native Ministry, with Lutheran Indian Ministries specifically, is a beautiful mix of Lutheran outreach.

Depending on where you look, it's:

* Campus Ministry
* Lutheran Education
* Health and Nutrition Education
* Leadership and Community Development
* Prison Ministry
* Seminary Training
* Youth and Church Missions Mobilization
* Men, Women, & Children Bible Studies
* Vacation Bible School
* Abuse Recovery & Counseling
* Hospital, Respite, & Shut-In Outreach
* Food & Clothing Distribution  
  ... with a little bit of an International Missions feel to top it off.

Are you more confused?
