# Mighty God - Saturday, December 19 (Advent 2020)

Dec 19

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

![](images/8fe5f2ec-image-asset.png)

## For to us a child is born, to us a son is given… And he will be called… Mighty God… [Isaiah 9:6](https://my.bible.com/bible/111/ISA.9.6)

**Mighty** - גִּבּוֹר (gibbowr) - valiant, strongest, champion, chief

**God** - אֵל (‘êl) - God, power, greatness, the Almighty

We have the valiant champion, the strongest power, the Almighty on our side. As we get closer to Christmas Day, let us continue to remember who it is we are celebrating. It is not merely a baby in a manger, but the Savior himself in the flesh.

Take today to catch up on any missed days, and reflect on the Mighty God who came to earth and humbled himself to save us.
