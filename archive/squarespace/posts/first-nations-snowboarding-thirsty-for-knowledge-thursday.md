# First Nations Snowboarding - Thirsty (for Knowledge) Thursday

Jan 19

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Canada’s First Nations Snowboard Team sponsors young Indigenous athletes. Team member Jonny Williams uses the sport to connect more deeply with his land and culture. He’s also part of a growing environmental youth movement, keeping oil and gas development out of his community.
