## For to us a child is born, to us a son is given… And he will be called… Everlasting Father… [Isaiah 9:6](https://my.bible.com/bible/111/ISA.9.6)

**Everlasting** - עַד (‘ad) - eternally, perpetually, ancient

**Father** - אָב (‘ab) - principal, chief, originator

The baby we celebrated yesterday is also the Everlasting Father, the Creator of the Heavens and the Earth, who existed at the beginning and will be forever.

Take today to catch up on any missed days, and reflect on the Everlasting Father who created the world and each one of us personally, and loves us as His children.
