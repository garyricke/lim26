# You can't stop the Holy Spirit

[Arizona/Phoenix](/news-notes/category/Arizona%2FPhoenix)

Nov 21

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Kevin Maulson just keeps expanding his footprint in Phoenix and the surrounding areas.

Reaching out to organizations, government programs, homeless shelters, tribal agencies, he has become a point person for Native peoples in need.

The last few weeks, he and his wife, Kathy, had to take multiple trips in their van to get everyone to Bible study on Sunday!

![](images/b3cc46ff-image-asset.jpeg)

Van load #1. “I think we need a bus!” joked Kevin.

![](images/0b698cea-image-asset.jpeg)

Bible study is getting crowded. Kevin writes: “We had packed house. Thank you to our brothers and sisters at New Vision for adding the great addition to our meal. I was a bit worried but as always God knew we needed more. Great bible study and church service. Two trips in our 15 passenger van today. Praise God!”

Kevin is also continuing his work on Gila River Reservation.

Recently Kevin wrote: Great day at Blackwater church with our brothers and sisters on Gila River reservation. Sister Darlene first time leading Bible study. God is doing some amazing work. Thank you to the church members for the great Potluck and fellowship after the service.
