# Who Do We Obey?

[Nebraska/Winnebago](/news-notes/category/Nebraska%2FWinnebago)

Nov 6

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Rev. Ricky Jacob, pastor to the Winnebago people in Nebraska, has just released a [7-day series of devotions](http://kticradio.com/) related to our upcoming presidential elections.

Who do we obey? We obey our Lord, our Heavenly Father.

Regardless of how you feel about the upcoming election, our candidates, and the outcome, our duty is to the Lord, and we put our trust in Him and His will.

Enjoy!
