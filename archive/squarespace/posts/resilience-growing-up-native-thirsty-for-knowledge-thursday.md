# Resilience Growing Up Native - Thirsty (for Knowledge) Thursday

Jul 20

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

In this moving, [10-minute profile](https://www.theatlantic.com/video/index/513014/growing-up-native-american/), a Shoshone elder from Wyoming reflects on his childhood and his time fighting in Vietnam.

Philbert McLeod is featured in the new documentary [*What Was Ours*](http://www.pbs.org/independentlens/films/what-was-ours/), about Native Americans’ fight to reclaim their missing artifacts from Chicago’s Field Museum. You can stream the full documentary [on the PBS website](http://www.pbs.org/independentlens/videos/what-was-ours-full-film/).
