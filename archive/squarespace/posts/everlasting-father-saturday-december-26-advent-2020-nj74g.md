## For to us a child is born, to us a son is given… And he will be called… the Prince of Peace… [Isaiah 9:6](https://my.bible.com/bible/111/ISA.9.6)

**Prince** - שַׂר (sar) - ruler, prince, steward, keeper

**Of Peace** - שָׁלוֹם (shalowm) - safety, health, completeness, wellness

As we celebrate the beginning of a new year, we pray the Prince of Peace would bless you with safety, wellness, and a completeness that can only come from Him.
