Because you recognize the importance of this work, LIM has been able to send our Sacred Story team to Montana twice, in the past 4 months. It is our prayer to bring Sacred Story to all seven Montana reservations. With your help, we will do that.

# [The Montana Healing Project (times 2)](/news-notes/the-montana-healing-project-times-2)

Last fall, Lutheran Indian Ministries was invited by a local Native pastor to visit two reservations in Montana to share and train local leaders in Sacred Ground.

In January, our team took their second trip to Montana and were blown away by the receptive response from the group who showed up the first day.
