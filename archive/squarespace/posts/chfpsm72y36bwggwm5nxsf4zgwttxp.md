Another Offensive Political Comment |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| |  | | --- | | Two Bison | | Yet again, a political figure has offended the Native American people. This one, you may have already heard about.    Last week, [Hillary Clinton commented](http://www.foxnews.com/politics/2016/05/01/clinton-s-off-reservation-remark-angers-native-americans-forces-twitter-response.html) that she had dealt with many men who had "gone off the reservation." She has since apologized.    So, why, is this comment offensive? [Find out here.](http://www.npr.org/sections/codeswitch/2014/06/29/326690947/should-saying-someone-is-off-the-reservation-be-off-limits)    While you're at here, here's [why native mascots are offensive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HTBT-_F6oYw).    One more point:    Cultural appropriation, the adoption or use of elements of one culture by members of a different culture, has been a hot topic lately and covers everything from [clothing lines selling headdresses](http://www.today.com/style/free-people-accused-appropriating-native-american-culture-festival-line-t85821) to [makeup product lines](http://www.refinery29.com/2016/04/108902/mac-vibe-tribe-cultural-appropriation-controversy).    [Here's why Native Americans find wearing headdresses offensive](http://nativeappropriations.com/2010/04/but-why-cant-i-wear-a-hipster-headdress.html). | |

| |  | | --- | | Recent High School Grad Solves Water Problem | |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| |  | | --- | | first nations home | | North Spirit Lake First Nation, located about 500 miles northwest of Thunder Bay, Ontario, has been under a boil water advisory for 14 years. A new program from the Northern Chiefs Council, asked people to take the initiative and the responsibility for providing clean water in their own communities, and 19-year-old Quentin Rae has stepped up.   ---  *"I have to take care of the plant, make sure chlorine gets in the water to clean the bacteria," said Rae with a smile during his first week on the job at the end of April.  "I just graduated last year and they gave me the job."* [Read the full article here.](http://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/thunder-bay/teen-first-nation-drinking-water-1.3563110)   ---    Once Rae is fully trained, the boil advisory can finally be lifted. | |
