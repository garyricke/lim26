The presentation will delve into the transformative power of faith, healing, and worship, as well as the importance of fostering a relationship with God.

Katherine Gottlieb, born to an Alaska Native mother and a Filipino father, grew up amidst the hardships of alcoholism, domestic violence, abuse, and neglect. Marrying and becoming a mother at the age of sixteen, she embarked on a personal mission to break the cycle of silence that plagued her and many others. Fueled by a resilient faith, she has refused to let her difficult past define her; instead, her life is a testament to determination, ongoing struggle, and enduring hope.

This event is open to all individuals who are 18 years old and above. We look forward to seeing you there!
