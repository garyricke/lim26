# THANK YOU!

Dec 26

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

## Thank you for taking this Advent journey with us

This ministry is ripe with potential and opportunity. The chance to serve God and share His Good News with our Native brothers and sisters is a special one.

If through this series of devotions, you have felt God place Native Ministry on your heart and would like to join us in sharing the Gospel with Native people, discipling Native leaders to share the Gospel with their brothers and sisters, and addressing social sufferings in a way that values Native cultures, please [make a gift to Lutheran Indian Ministries.](/devotion-donate)

[I want to make a difference](/devotion-donate)

As you enter the Epiphany season, please continue to hold this ministry, and those we strive to serve, in your prayers.

### We look forward to seeing you again during Lent.

**If you have any questions before then, please don’t hesitate to [contact us](/contact).**
