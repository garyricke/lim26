# Healing The Wounds of Trauma

A Small Group for Bible-based Trauma Healing

![](images/dfb7f98b-healingwounds-trauma-healing-institute-logo-american-bible.png)

* Are you or someone you know struggling with grief?
* Do you have heart wounds that haven’t healed?
* Are You struggling to forgive someone who hurt you?
* Want to be better equipped to help others experiencing pain and suffering?
* Wonder what God word says about your emotional pain?
* Has it been hard to find people who really care or understand?

* About

  This healing group uses the proven and time-tested model of the Trauma Healing Institute at American Bible Society. Using applied Scripture and best mental health principles, we can help you address spiritual and emotional wounds caused by trauma of violence, serious accidents, and abuse. The group will be led by facilitators who are trained in Bible-based trauma healing.
* What You'll Learn

  • Learn about trauma and its effects

  • Explore trauma or loss you may be carrying

  • Release some of the emotional pain you carry • Help you get unstuck

  • Build resilience for the future
* Material

  The group will use the book Healing the Wounds of Trauma: How the Church Can Help. It is in use in over one hundred countries with more than 194 language groups in Africa, Asia, the Pacific, and the Americas. To learn more about the model, the Trauma Healing Institute, and its work in equipping and supporting a global community of workers in trauma care, please visit http://www.TraumaHealingInstitute.org.

Part 4

Healing the Wounded Trauma, initiated in Africa and now in over 100 countries, empowers communities globally, including Alaska, to understand and address trauma through culturally informed, faith-based perspectives, changing perceptions on the Bible and God's approach to healing.

Part 2

This approach highlights grief as an integral and natural aspect of love, encouraging understanding and connection through the acknowledgment of loss, inspired by biblical examples like Jesus' grief, thereby fostering deeper community support and personal healing.

Part 5

This program addresses common misconceptions about God's role in human suffering, emphasizing His constant compassion and equipping individuals to provide real, empathetic support, thereby revealing God's love through human connections in times of trauma.

Part 7

Recognizing Alaska's struggles with high rates of domestic violence and child neglect, this initiative emphasizes the need for communal education and action to improve family wellness and safeguard the future of the state's children, both in Alaska Native and non-native communities.

Part 3

Participants in our program gain a deep understanding of God's compassion for the wounded, a sense of community with others who have experienced trauma, and a renewed perspective on the Bible's relevance to healing and personal growth.

Part 6

This program offers free training for those who have completed a healing group, equipping them to facilitate their own groups, thereby enabling them to demonstrate God's healing power and aid in transforming how people view trauma and God's presence in their own and others' lives.
