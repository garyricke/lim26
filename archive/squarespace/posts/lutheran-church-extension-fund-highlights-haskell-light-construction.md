# Lutheran Church Extension Fund Highlights Haskell LIGHT Construction

[Kansas/Haskell LIGHT](/news-notes/category/Kansas%2FHaskell+LIGHT)

Jan 9

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Lutheran Indian Ministries' Haskell LIGHT Campus Ministry in Lawrence, Kansas, is currently featured in an online article by Lutheran Church Extension Fund (LCEF). Click [here](http://www.lcef.org/headlines/?nHeadlineID=298) to read the story.
In the article, John Davito, project manager for the construction of our new campus ministry facility, shares his enthusiasm for overseeing the work with the help of Laborers For Christ volunteers and others.

Will and Patricia Main, co-directors at our campus ministry, explain the importance of their ministry and the need to expand as they reach out to more than 1,000 Native university students attending Haskell Indian Nations University in Lawrence every semester.

For more information about our Haskell LIGHT Campus Ministry building campaign, contact [Lutheran Indian Ministries](/contacts "Contact") today!
