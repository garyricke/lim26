# What to Watch: Injunuity

Jun 16

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Different than anything else this series has highlighted. Shorter and animated, but no less powerful (maybe even more so!).

This week's featured film is: [Injunuity](http://americanarchive.org/catalog/cpb-aacip_508-nv9959d319)

*As part of Vision Maker Media's "40 years, 40 films, 40 weeks" program, one archived PBS film will be re-released each week. Watch them while they are available!*

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/1665777701611-BR7V3770UG13OZDT0QLG/image-asset.png)](http://americanarchive.org/catalog/cpb-aacip_508-nv9959d319)

A documentary film using interviews and archival footage to show the vital role a small group of Navajo Marines played in the Pacific during World War II. Includes 1940s archival footage of Navajo life as well as scenes of World War II. Featured are interviews with Navajo Chairman Peter MacDonald, artist and scholar Carl Gorman, and Taos artist R.C. Gorman. Received a special Presidential commendation by Ronald Reagan. Produced by Tom McCarthy in 1998.
