# Creating an Awareness of Jesus - Will Main's Story

[Kansas/Haskell LIGHT](/news-notes/category/Kansas%2FHaskell+LIGHT)

May 12

Written By [LIM](/news-notes?author=568401b704acd3cdb4a44043)

“For I know the plans I have for you, declares the LORD …” (Jeremiah 29:11)
Rev. Will Main *(Dakota Sioux)* never imagined that one day God would bring him to faith in Christ—thanks, in part, to the faithful witness of his wife, Patricia *(Cree)*—let alone set him on a path to become a pastor to Native students attending Haskell Indian Nations University in Lawrence, Kansas.

In this video, Will describes how he and Patricia reach out to students on a daily basis through Haskell LIGHT Campus Ministry. Whether it is to provide extra clothing, a meal or counseling, their intention for the students is to “create an awareness of Jesus” in the process. From there, they know God will do the rest.
