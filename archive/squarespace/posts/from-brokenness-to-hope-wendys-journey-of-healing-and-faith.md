## An Example of Hope

On the next episode of The Sacred Connections Podcast, you'll hear from Wendy, a mother of eight from Anchorage, as she opens up about her journey through the Healing the Wounds of Trauma training.

Wendy shares the deep fear and vulnerability she faced, acknowledging the raw wounds that nearly destroyed her, and how the process of healing required confronting these pains head-on.

Through her story, Wendy reveals how she’s not only learning to heal herself but also hopes to lead her family and community toward restoration.

Join us as we explore the powerful journey of hope, healing, and leadership.

## Take Root In My Heart

Listen in as we dive into a powerful testimony of finding hope and healing through scripture.

Wendy shares how being surrounded by wisdom, the support of others, and access to God’s Word transformed her life during a time of deep searching.

She opens up about how the Bible became her constant companion, offering life-giving guidance that took root in her heart.

Tune in to hear how scripture became exactly what she needed to not only speak truth but live it in a way that brought healing and hope.

## Protector of Her People

> “You have come [...] for such a time as this.”

— Esther 4:14b

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/aef29266-0c66-4083-909d-9da4373a4760/Wendy_3_5_Favorite_Scripture_Esther--thumb.jpg)](https://player.vimeo.com/progressive_redirect/playback/1021871784/rendition/1080p/file.mp4?loc=external&log_user=0&signature=abdf06dc4296faf8851572790bb7f1123e28477931a2362671d6468445e658ea)

Listen as Wendy reflects on a powerful message from the book of Esther.

Wendy shares how the verse, “You are here for such a time as this,” speaks deeply to her, reminding us all that we are here for a reason, at this very moment, by God's design. She beautifully connects Esther's journey—from a young girl who lost everything to someone who protected God’s people—with the idea that no matter how humble our beginnings, we can all be part of something miraculous.

Join us as we explore the grace, humility, and purpose found in this timeless scripture.

## A Reassurance Beyond Compare

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/6b7c209f-c6b3-4ea1-82b0-d8fd579f6850/sacred-wendy-4-of-5-reassurance-thumb.jpg)](https://player.vimeo.com/progressive_redirect/playback/1023989669/rendition/720p/file.mp4?loc=external&log_user=0&signature=459eec20e9ad5ae77bcf3bcc7be7c6a7e99fafcf6fb2b272a38396b4b3a447ab)

Listen to Wendy share how her life has transformed since attending the healing group. She opens up about waking up each day with overwhelming sorrow and pain but describes the shift that came from seeing her journey laid out on paper and being encouraged through deep connections. She talks about the confidence she’s gained, realizing that, even when she felt lost, God had a purpose for her all along. Her story is a powerful reminder that healing and hope are possible, even in the darkest moments. Don’t miss this episode of reassurance and newfound strength.
