# Thank you for joining us (Lent) - Easter Monday

Apr 2

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

![](images/8d480206-image-asset.png)

I hope that you enjoyed your Lent devotions this year.

Hopefully, through these devotions, you were able to draw closer to your Savior while also learning more about Native Ministry through Lutheran Indian Ministries.

We would love for you to stick with us all year long with our weekly Monday Morning Devotions!

If you are signed up already, you should have received the first devotion of our new series, "The Bunny is Gon, So What's Next?," this morning.

If you didn't receive it, you can [read it here](http://lutheranindianministries.org/news/the-bunny-is-gone-so-whats-next-devotion-1-following-in-the-footsteps-of-the-apostles-monday-morning-devotion), and then sign up for the emails at the bottom of the devotion.

Thank you again for joining us this Lenten season. If you would like to do more to help reach Native peoples with the hope found in our Risen Lord, please prayerfully consider joining our [monthly TRIBE program](/tribe).

God's blessings in this new season of life and hope!

Tim Young Eagle (Pawnee)  
Executive Director, LIM
