# 2014 Lenten Devotions Now Available

[News from Brookfield](/news-notes/category/News+from+Brookfield)

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Our Lenten Devotions for 2014 are now available in PDF format on our Resources page. Be sure to download a copy and share the link with your congregation and friends! To receive printed copies while supplies last, please [contact](/contacts "Contact") our office or call at 888-783-5267. Click [Devotions](/resources/devotions "Devotions") to download a copy for local printing.
If you're unfamiliar with our devotions, you'll find that most of the stories are written by our ministry staff who often share how the Gospel transforms hearts and brings hope to our Native brothers and sisters. We pray you'll be blessed and encouraged as you follow the devotions for your own Lenten meditations.

Thank you for your prayers and support!
