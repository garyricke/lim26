Welcome to Sacred Connections: A dialogue on how the Gospel resonates within Native American and Alaska Native traditions and values, exploring themes of God’s creation, community, and healing through a blend of interviews, teachings, and storytelling.

At Lutheran Indian Ministries, we look forward to the day when all are in the light of Christ “…from every nation, tribe, people and language, standing before the throne and in front of the Lamb…”

Today’s discussion features Max Dolchok reflecting on his personal journey of transformation through faith.

Born in Alaska and of Athabaskan and Yupik heritage, Max shares his life story, and the pivotal change to reintroduce himself to faith with his wife's support.

He shares candidly about learning to express love, and the importance of family and humility over material possessions.

Max emphasizes the ongoing journey of learning to be more Christ-like, acknowledging his struggles.

He finds peace and guidance in Scripture, particularly Ephesians 6 verse 2-19, which underscores the importance of the full armor of God in navigating life's trials.

Max recognizes that the spiritual path embraces loved ones, highlighting the transformative power of God's love and forgiveness in overcoming life's challenges and finding peace and purpose through faith.
