# [TRUTH: what the world needs - Week 2 (Monday Morning Devotions)](/news-notes/truth-what-the-world-needs-week-2-monday-morning-devotions)

One of the truths Christians profess, which we just celebrated and is fresh in our minds, is of the virgin birth of God's Son, Jesus.

In the words of the Apostles' Creed, we profess Jesus ***"was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the virgin Mary."*** In the Nicene Creed, we profess Jesus *"****was incarnate by the Holy Spirit of the virgin Mary and was made man;"*** And in the Athanasian Creed, we profess Jesus is God, ***"begotten from the substance of the Father before all ages; and He is man, born from the substance of His mother in this age."***

The virgin birth of baby Jesus, celebrated on Christmas Day, has been brought into question by non-believers around the world. We turn to God's Sacred Word as our source of Transcendent Truth.
