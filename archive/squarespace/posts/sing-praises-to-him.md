# Sing Praises to Him - Winston Wilson's Story

[Washington/Neah Bay](/news-notes/category/Washington%2FNeah+Bay)

Jun 5

Written By [LIM](/news-notes?author=568401b704acd3cdb4a44043)

Winston Wilson (Cowlitz) and his wife, Connie, are passionate about singing praises to God. Music is a vital part of their ministry to the Makah Natives in Neah Bay, Washington.
*“Sing to him, sing praise to him; tell of all his wonderful acts”* (Psalm 105:2)

In this video, Winston identifies with many of the struggles his Native brothers and sisters face. Dedicated to sharing the Gospel with them through Word and Sacrament ministry and through his music, it is a joy to for him to see how God restores lives and relationships along the way.
