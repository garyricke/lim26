# What to Watch- Vis a Vis: Native Tongues

Dec 2

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

Looking for something good (and educational) to watch tonight?

As part of Vision Maker Media's "40 years, 40 films, 40 weeks" program, one archived PBS film will be re-released each week. So, watch them while they are available!

This week's featured film is: [Vis a Vis: Native Tongues](http://americanarchive.org/catalog/cpb-aacip_508-mw28912k28)

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/1665778824824-5RF9CQYA40MIP8VMWT4N/image-asset.jpeg)](http://americanarchive.org/catalog/cpb-aacip_508-mw28912k28)

Indigenous performing artists James Luna (Luise'o) and Ningali Lawford (Walmajarri) compare perspectives on life and society, using dialogue via satellite, scenes of their performances, and video diaries to inform the conversation.
