# Trunk or Treating Fun at Makah Lutheran

[Washington/Neah Bay](/news-notes/category/Washington%2FNeah+Bay)

Oct 31

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

![](images/6b197f1b-image-asset.jpeg)

Ben and Natalie (Makah) Maxson held another successful Trunk or Treat in Neah Bay on October 28th.

Trunk or Treat is a safe and fun way for kids to trick-or-treat between decorated car trunks. Ben and Natalie partnered with the Neah Bay Community Gym to hold the event in their parking lot. Over the course of a couple hours, they hosted close to 250 children from the community who participated in trick-or-treating, apple bobbing, and a variety of games, shopped the pumpkin patch, and enjoyed hot cider, cocoa, and corn on the cob.

![](images/839a1d32-image-asset.jpeg)

Garden themed trunk at Makah Lutheran’s Trunk or Treat in Neah Bay, Washington.

As always, the theme was [Generosity is Meant to Be Shared](https://www.lutheranindianministries.org/news/generosity-is-meant-to-be-shared?rq=generosity), and it certainly was.
