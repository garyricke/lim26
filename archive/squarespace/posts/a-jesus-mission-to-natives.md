# A Mission to Natives.

Dec 21

Written By [Guest User](/news-notes?author=6839f4dcc52c4707a9ee7c66)

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/62389dbdc8d62b0755bf6d44/1665777105446-SUDD44L2CHHZ77XUGLDP/image-asset.png)

Your mission, should you choose to join us... Proclaim. Disciple. Heal.

Over the course of the last year, our ministry staff has been hard at work reaching Native communities with the love of God and the hope that comes through Jesus Christ. The Holy Spirit is moving on reservations and in urban areas. Below is just a portion of the great things happening and a taste of what is to come.

For all the victories for Christ and all the opportunities we see before us, we thank the Holy Spirit for the wisdom and understanding He gives us as we follow God's will and follow Jesus' mission. And we thank you! Without your support, both in prayer and financial gifts, we wouldn't be here. It is through your passion and commitment, we are able to reach families with the Gospel and assist them in the healing of their past trauma.

We couldn't be more thrilled to share with you how your gifts have helped Native individuals and to show you the work you can help us continue in the New Year.
