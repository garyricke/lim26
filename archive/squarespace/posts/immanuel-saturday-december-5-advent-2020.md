## Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign: The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and will call him Immanuel. [Isaiah 7:14](https://my.bible.com/bible/111/ISA.7.14)

**Immanuel** - עִמָּנוּאֵל (Immanuw’el) - God with us and beside us

In this time of waiting and anticipation, we are reassured that God is always with us.

Take today to catch up on any missed days, and reflect on the truth that God is with us at all times.
