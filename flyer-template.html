Fairbanks, Alaska Healing Groups

What does the Bible say about healing and restoration? Join us for a free, six-session group rooted in Scripture and walked together.

FREE

Healing Groups are a free, confidential gathering developed by the American Bible Society’s Trauma Healing Institute. We use Scripture to show how God’s Word heals and restores after trauma and loss — and how we can walk alongside others on their healing journey.

Six sessions, walked together

  1. What is a wound of the heart?
  2. What happens when someone is grieving?
  3. If God loves us, why do we suffer?
  4. Bringing our pain to the cross
  5. What can help our heart wounds heal?
  6. How can we forgive others?
All adults 18+ welcome Lunch provided Childcare not provided

A safe place to begin

You’ll sit in a small circle, read together, listen, and rest. There is no pressure to share. This is space to bring what you carry — and meet a God who is near to the brokenhearted.
