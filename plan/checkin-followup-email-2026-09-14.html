Jaylene's question — clicks vs impressions

An impression is your page appearing in someone's Google results, whether or not they noticed it. A click is them choosing it. August: 13,400 impressions, 185 clicks — about 1.4 in every 100, which is normal for a site this new. The number to watch is impressions: it's Google's confidence in us, and it should climb every month from here.
