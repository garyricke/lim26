## Recommended itinerary

day. It saves nothing — housing is already covered through the 24th — and it

Fairbanks near midnight body-clock time.

Arriving Sunday also buys a real buffer. A missed connection in Seattle on
Sunday is an inconvenience; the same miss on Monday morning means you walk in
halfway through day one.

## Packing and conditions

Late September in Fairbanks runs roughly **46°F days, mid-30s nights**, dropping
through the week. Frost is normal; snow is possible but not likely. Layers, a
real jacket, and waterproof shoes — this is not Chicago-in-September weather.

Daylight is near the equinox and falling fast, about **12½ hours and shrinking
roughly seven minutes a day**. Sunrise around 7:30, dark by 8:30.

**You will be there during aurora season.** It opens in late August and late
September is one of the better windows — dark enough to see, not yet cold enough
to be miserable standing outside. You're bringing camera gear anyway. Zion is on
the west side away from downtown light, and the Chena Ridge and Cleary Summit
areas north of town are the usual viewing spots. Worth a night if the forecast
cooperates.
