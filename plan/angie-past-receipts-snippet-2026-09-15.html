Getting thank-yous to the donors who gave between 15 August and today

The gifts themselves are all recorded — it's only the email that never went out. Three ways to fix that, easiest first:

1. Ask Blackbaud first. Add one line to the case:

The sending address is corrected and receipts are working. Can you re-send the Online Express thank-you email for all gifts on this form from 8/15/2026 through 9/15/2026?

They may be able to re-trigger them in bulk from their side. If yes, you're done.

2. If they can't: send them from Raiser's Edge. Every online gift already landed in the database as a gift record. Pull the online gifts for that date range (Online Express → Donations shows the list, or a gift query on gift date with Online Express as the source), then run the same donor-acknowledgement mail you'd use for check gifts and have it go out by email. One caution: Blackbaud may have marked these gifts as “acknowledged” even though the email failed, so filter by date, not by acknowledged status, or the list will come up empty.

3. If the list is short, do it by hand. A month of online gifts is often a manageable number. A personal email from you with the gift date, amount and designation — and a line saying the automatic receipt failed and you wanted to thank them yourself — is the version donors remember most warmly. If you want, forward me the list and I'll draft the note.

Whichever route, the donors should get their tax-receipt information: date, amount, LIM's EIN 39-1506889, and that no goods or services were provided. Routes 1 and 2 handle that automatically; for route 3 I'll put it in the template.
