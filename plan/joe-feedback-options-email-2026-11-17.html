Other easy ways that work well

  • Talk it through. Record a voice memo on your phone while you read, and email me the recording. Several of my clients do this. On an iPhone, Voice Memos also makes a written transcript you can copy and paste into an email.
  • One long email. Just type your notes as they come. They don’t need to be tidy.
  • Send your own documents. If you want to change any of your original documents, edit them however you like and send them back. I’ll feed them in, and the curriculum will be updated to match.
  • Pen and paper. Print a section, write on it, and send me phone photos of the pages.

Whichever you choose, a part number or heading with each note (“Part 3, the story”) helps me find the right spot.
