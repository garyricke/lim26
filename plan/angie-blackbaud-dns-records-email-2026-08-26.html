Skip the DMARC one — this part matters

We already have a DMARC record on our domain, and it's working. Blackbaud says the same thing themselves, right on the screen you sent — it's the grey italic line most people scroll past:

From your screenshot — Blackbaud's own instruction to skip.

The reason to be careful here: a domain is allowed exactly one DMARC record. Add a second and they don't combine — they cancel, and DMARC stops working for everything, staff Outlook email included. So that one record could turn a donation-receipt problem into an all-staff email problem. We leave it alone.

One small thing on the values you pasted: the text under _dmarc is actually the DKIM key pasted a second time. Easy slip, and no harm done — that's the record we're skipping anyway.
