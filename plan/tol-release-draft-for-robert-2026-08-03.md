## The one question Robert needs to answer

**Replace Edgenuity, or add LIM alongside it?**

The draft above *replaces* Edgenuity. That is right if LIM is running the 2026
gathering on its own.

If Edgenuity is still involved — curriculum, trainers, licensing — they may
still need to be named, and the line should read *"Lutheran Indian Ministries,
Edgenuity, and Tree of Life Gathering."* Removing a party who is still involved
leaves them unprotected and could sour the relationship.
