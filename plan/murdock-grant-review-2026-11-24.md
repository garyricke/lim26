Joe's plan (email "grant", 23 Nov): LOI in the Mar 1–29, 2027 window. Category: Health → Abuse Prevention & Healing. Hire a director of marketing/development to grow Pacific NW giving. About $500k, with $100k from LIM. Tech and training included. 2:1 ROI.

## Verified against murdocktrust.org/grant-application-process/guidelines-for-grantseekers
- Staff grants decline over 3 years: 100/67/33%. Max 1.0 FTE.
- "The Trust will only fund an organization's first fundraising position."
- Expected ROI: new funds raised in Year 4 at least double the position's cost (anticipated, not a hard rule).
- HQ outside AK/ID/MT/OR/WA is allowed, but at least 50% of project impact must be in the Northwest. LIM is HQ'd in Brookfield, WI. Precedent: We Are Healers (CO), $312,500 for a development role serving Native youth in the PNW.
- Minimum project budget $200k. Minimum request $100k.
- Not funded: positions that existed in the last 5 years, existing staff, admin roles, anyone hired before the site visit.
- 2027 Period 1: LOI Mar 1–29 → decision by May 28 → full application May 28–Jun 23 → site visit → Trustees decide Dec 2027 → realistic hire in 2028.
- Portal: Submittable. An eligibility quiz unlocks the LOI.
- Contacts: grants@murdocktrust.org, (360) 694-8415. Health: Kim Newman (Sr PO). Faith Formation: Jeremy White (Sr PO).

## LOI fields (SPv3 PDF)
- Organization details: EIN, legal name, address, head of organization, regional contact. Mission, year founded.
- Programs and services: 300 words.
- Geography served, with the share in AK/WA/OR/ID/MT: 300 words.
- Operating budget.
- Grant type. Project description: 300 words. Total cost and request (both 3-year totals). Use of funds: 300 words. Dates. FTE added. Percent government funding.
- Fundraising questions: is an organized donor-development effort under way? Private contributions last year ($).
- Target population: 300 words. Measurable outcomes: 300–350 words. Impact: 300 words.
- Prior contact with the Trust. Anything else. Hours spent writing.

## Full application (invited only)
- Online sections: organization, board leadership, project information, outcomes.
- Uploads: Project Budget Template – Staff; Revenue & Expense Projections; board list template; IRS 509(a)/170(b) documentation.
- Each document max 2 pages. Excel preferred.
- Narrative questions are visible only after the LOI is approved.

## Risks in the current plan
1. **$500k is high.** A $500k grant implies about a $250k/yr role. 2026 development-staff grants ran $137k–$394k. Size it at about $250–350k.
2. **No 1:1 or 2:1 match is published.** LIM's contribution is the declining share, then the full cost from Year 4.
3. **First-fundraising-position rule.** Has LIM ever had a paid development role? Any role in the last 5 years is disqualifying.
4. **The 50% Northwest-impact case** has to be made for a WI-based organization.
5. **Category.** The Trust may file LIM under Faith Formation rather than Health. Ask grants@ before March.
6. **Training money** isn't allowed in a staff grant. Only new startup technology costs are.
