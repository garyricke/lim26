How to do this yourself next time

No apology needed, the button is easy to miss on purpose. Here it is, step by step.

1. Find the “A” button. It floats in the bottom-right corner of every page and follows you as you scroll, so it's never at the very bottom of the content. It's faint until you hover over it. If the cookie notice is showing, close that first. It sits right on top of the button.

Bottom-right corner of the Healing Groups page.

2. Enter the admin password: lim2026admin

Type the password and press Enter.

3. Choose “Events Manager” from the menu that slides in.

The first time on a new computer it may ask for the site password, limhope. After that it remembers you.

4. Scroll to the list at the bottom and press “Edit” on the group you want to change.

Every healing group on the website is in this list.

5. Change the wording. The form fills in with that group. “Date label” is the big text on the card, “Description” is the paragraph under it. The card on the right updates as you type, so you can see exactly what visitors will see.

Left: the fields. Right: a live preview of the card.

6. Publish. Type the publish password, ihavehope, and press the green button. The website updates within a minute or two.

Nothing goes live until you press this button, so you can't break anything by looking around.
