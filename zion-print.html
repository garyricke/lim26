Printing them right

  • Print at 100% / Actual Size. Turn off “Fit to page” or “Shrink oversized pages” in the print dialog — otherwise you get a white band around the edge instead of color running to the trim.
  • Turn on background graphics if your printer asks. Print the downloaded PDF rather than the web page and the colors come out right on their own.
  • The half-page sheet cuts once, horizontally down the middle. A print shop will guillotine a stack in one pass.
