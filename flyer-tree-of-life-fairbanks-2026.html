Fairbanks, Alaska Tree of Life Gathering

Three days on a Christ-centred healing journey. Native-led.

FREE

Behind our eyes, every one of us carries a story that has shaped us — and most of us have never told it. The Tree of Life Gathering follows Jesus from birth through the cross, because Jesus knew rejection, loss, and trauma too. You sit in a small group with trained Native leaders and share only what you choose to share.

What the three days hold

  1. Teaching from a presenter who shares their own story
  2. Small groups, with two trained leaders each
  3. Space to name grief, abuse, neglect, loneliness
  4. An evening celebration on the final day
All are welcome · 18+ No cost to attend Housing help available

You don’t need a story to come

You may not know your story yet, and that is fine — people find it here. Nobody is pressured to share. What people say afterwards is: I didn’t realise I had a story.
