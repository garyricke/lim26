Printing them right

  • Print at 100% / Actual Size. Turn off “Fit to page” or “Shrink oversized pages” — otherwise you get a white band around the edge instead of color running to the trim.
  • Print the downloaded PDF, not the web page. The PDF already has the background colors baked in, so nothing depends on your printer’s settings.
  • Handing them out? Plain 80 lb text is fine and cheap. For a notice board, 100 lb gloss cover holds up better.
  • Want a different angle? Tell Gary what the room needs and he’ll build a fourth.
Every fact on these flyers is drawn from the About page — if something changes there, tell Gary so the handouts stay in step.
